Set up your AI helper

About 2 minutes · do this once
›

Your AI helper is the AI app on your phone (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, any of them). The message below tells it about your business and this plan, so it can walk you through every job one step at a time.

  1. Tap Copy the helper message below.
  2. Open your AI app and start a new chat.
  3. Press and hold in the message box, tap Paste, then send it.
  4. It will ask which job you want to do. From then on, just answer it.

Use the same chat every night so it remembers where you left off. If you lose the chat, paste the message into a new one and tell it which jobs are done.

2

Make sure you can edit your Google listing

About 5 minutes
›

Most local customers find a contractor on Google Maps before they ever see a website. Your listing is there now, but it needs a few fixes.

You'll need: the Google (Gmail) account you used when you set up the business.
  1. Open the Google app or Chrome, signed in to that account.
  2. Search Bristol Builders.
  3. Look for your business with buttons like Edit profile or Your business above the results.
  4. See it? You're set. Tap Done.

Don't see it? You may be signed in with a different account, or the listing may need to be claimed. Screenshot what you see and send it to your helper.

3

Change your category to "General contractor"

About 5 minutes
›

Right now Google files you under "Remodeler," so you don't come up when people search "general contractor near me." This is the biggest single fix.

  1. From your listing, tap Edit profile.
  2. Find Business category.
  3. Change the main category to General contractor.
  4. Under additional categories, add any that fit your work, like Deck builder or Siding contractor.
  5. Tap Save.

Google sometimes takes a few days to review changes. That's normal.

4

Check your map pin and towns

About 10 minutes
›

Your pin may not be in Bristol. Google shows you to people near your pin and in the towns you list.

  1. In Edit profile, open Location (it may be called Business location or Address).
  2. Check that the pin is on your real spot. If not, drag it there and save.
  3. Find Service area and add the towns you actually work in: Bristol, Middlebury, Vergennes, Monkton, New Haven, Lincoln, Starksboro, and others.
  4. If you work from home and don't want your address public, your helper can show you how to hide it and show only the service area.

6

Ask 3 past customers for a review

About 10 minutes
›

Reviews decide who gets the call. Your listing has 0. The nearest competitors have 8 and 24. Three reviews from happy customers puts you on the board.

  1. On your listing, tap Ask for reviews (or Get more reviews). Copy the link it gives you.
  2. Pick 3 past customers you know were happy.
  3. Copy the text below, paste it into a text message, and put in their name and your link.
Hi [name], it's Dakota from Bristol Builders. Thanks again for having me out. If you were happy with the work, a quick Google review would help a small local business more than you'd think: [review link]. Thanks!

From now on, send this after every finished job, within a couple of days. It's the most valuable 30 seconds in this whole plan. Reply to every review you get, even just "Thanks, it was a good job to work on."

7

Add 10 job photos to Google

About 10 minutes
›

People hire with their eyes. Photos of your real work beat anything written.

  1. On your listing, tap Add photo (or Photos in Edit profile).
  2. Pick 10 of your best job photos from your phone.
  3. Upload. That's it.
On every job from now on, take 3 photos:

2. After, wide, from the same spot
3. A close-up of the detail work

8

Update the words on your website

2–3 nights, about 15 minutes each
›

This is the biggest job, so it's split up. Your AI helper already has all the new words. It'll ask you a few questions first, then walk you through the ZenBusiness editor one section at a time.

  1. Tell your helper: "I'm ready for job 8."
  2. Answer its questions about your business.
  3. Log in to ZenBusiness the way you normally do. Your helper will guide you from there. Send it a screenshot whenever you're unsure.
  4. Do one section per night: headline first, then services, then the rest.

Changes don't go live until you hit Publish in ZenBusiness, so you can't break anything by looking around.

Optional: Efficiency Vermont

Efficiency Vermont keeps a network of contractors it refers customers to for energy-related work like windows, doors, and fixing drafts. Ask your helper to look up how to apply.

Three rules
Only say what's true.

If anything in the ready-made text isn't how you work, change it or skip it.

Your passwords stay with you.

Never give your AI helper a password or code. Type those in yourself.

None of this costs money.

If anything asks you to pay or upgrade, stop and ask your helper first.
